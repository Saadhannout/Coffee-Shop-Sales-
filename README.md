# Coffee Shop Sales Analysis – Excel Dashboard

## Overview
An interactive dashboard analyzing coffee shop sales across three store locations
(Astoria, Hell's Kitchen, Lower Manhattan), covering sales, footfall, product
categories, order sizes, and traffic by hour and weekday. Filters are available
by month and day.

## Dashboard Scope
* Total sales: $698,812
* Total footfall: 149,116
* Average bill per person: $4.69
* Average orders per person: 1.44
* Store locations: 3
* Months in the filter: January – June

## Dashboard Features
* Order activity by hour of day
* Sales distribution by product category
* Order distribution by size
* Footfall and total sales by store location
* Top 5 products by sales
* Footfall by day of the week

## Key Findings
* Coffee accounted for 39% of sales, followed by Bakery at 12%.
* Barista Espresso was the top product by sales ($91,406), followed by
  Brewed Chai tea ($77,082) and Hot chocolate ($72,416).
* Hell's Kitchen had the highest sales ($236,511) and footfall (50,735).
  Lower Manhattan had the lowest on both ($230,057 and 47,782).
* Despite the lowest footfall, Lower Manhattan had the highest average bill
  per person (about $4.81 vs $4.66 in Hell's Kitchen and $4.59 in Astoria).
* Order activity peaked in the morning (8–10 AM) and dropped after 11 AM.
* Footfall was fairly even across the week: Friday was the busiest day (21,701)
  and Saturday the slowest (20,510).
* About 30% of orders had no defined size, which limits size-level analysis.

## Important Note
Findings describe patterns in the data, not verified causes.
